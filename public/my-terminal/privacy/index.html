My Terminal

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

In short: My Terminal collects nothing and sends nothing. It contains no networking code, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no account and no AI. Everything you type, every command and everything it prints stays on your Mac.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet or to any server — not even to check for updates.
  • It does not collect, store or share personal data, usage data or diagnostics.
  • It does not send your commands, output, files or clipboard anywhere, and has no AI features.

Programs you run yourself inside the terminal (for example ssh, git push or curl) can of course use the network — exactly as they would in Apple's Terminal. That is your command, not the app.

What stays on your Mac

  • Settings in ~/Library/Preferences/dev.smerticariu.myterminal.plist, including the tabs and folders reopened after a restart.
  • Workflows and custom themes in ~/Library/Application Support/My Terminal.
  • A completion cache in ~/Library/Caches/My Terminal, and a temporary list of your shell aliases (readable only by you, deleted when the shell exits).
  • Command history is your shell's own file (e.g. ~/.zsh_history); the app only reads it to suggest commands.

Protections built in

  • Secure Keyboard Entry while a program asks for a password, so other apps can't read your keystrokes.
  • Recognised secrets (tokens, keys, passwords) are hidden on screen and when copying, unless you choose otherwise.
  • Notifications for long commands show only the program's name, never its arguments.

Permissions

macOS may ask to let My Terminal access folders such as Documents or Desktop. That access is used only by the commands you run, on your Mac.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
