Getting started

What does JiggleMate do?

It keeps your Mac awake and active while you're away from it. It waits until you've been idle for a while, then keeps the Mac active, and stops the instant you touch the mouse or keyboard again.

Where is the app after I open it?

JiggleMate lives in the menu bar (top right of the screen). Click its icon to open the panel; right-click it for quick timers, pause and mode options. The default shortcut ⌃⌘J turns it on or off from anywhere.

Does it need any permission?

No. Out of the box JiggleMate keeps your Mac awake using standard macOS power management — no permission needed (the panel shows Keep-awake mode). To also move the pointer, scroll and press a silent key, allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility. JiggleMate uses this only to post pointer, scroll and key events; it never reads other apps or what you type.

I allowed the permission but it still says Keep-awake mode.

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, make sure JiggleMate is switched on, then quit and reopen JiggleMate. If it's listed twice (for example after reinstalling), remove both entries with the – button and allow it again.

Modes & activity

What's the difference between Classic, Natural and Silent?

Classic makes a quick small move. Natural moves along human-like curves with varied speed and timing, and shifts schedule times by a few minutes each day. Silent moves nothing on screen and only sends activity events.

My chat app still shows me as away.

Some apps only count keyboard or scroll activity. Open the panel → Extra Activity and turn on Scroll and Key press (or apply the Stay Available preset). In Settings → Diagnostics, use Test Jiggle and check that the system idle time drops back to 0 — that is what most apps read. Also make sure the jiggle interval is shorter than the app's own away timeout.

Will it press keys or click things by accident?

The key press uses F18 by default, which has no function on a Mac. Clicks only happen if you turn them on, and only at the positions you choose. JiggleMate pauses whenever you use the mouse or keyboard.

Schedules & rules

How does the schedule work?

JiggleMate starts at the beginning of each time range and stops at the end. If you start or stop it manually, that choice is respected until the next scheduled change. Ranges can run past midnight.

What are "Only While" rules?

They make JiggleMate run only when something is true: chosen apps are running or in front, you're in a call, an external display is connected, the Mac is on power, the CPU is busy or audio is playing. Combine several with Any or All. Call detection only checks whether the microphone or camera is in use — it never records or opens them.

Why did it pause by itself?

The panel always shows the reason: the screen is locked, a blackout window, a rule isn't met, low battery, Low Power Mode, or the Mac is running hot. You can change each safeguard in Settings → System.

Automation

Can I control it from Shortcuts, Raycast or scripts?

Yes. The Shortcuts app has Start, Stop, Toggle, Pause and Get Status actions, and there's a Focus filter under System Settings → Focus. From Terminal, Raycast or Alfred use the URL scheme, for example open "jigglemate://start?minutes=30&mode=silent", jigglemate://stop, jigglemate://toggle, jigglemate://pause?minutes=15 or jigglemate://resume.

How do I restore my purchase on another Mac?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in JiggleMate and click Restore Purchases. Pro is shared with your Family Sharing group.

Other

What do I need to run it?

A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

How do I start it automatically?

Turn on Launch at login in Settings → General.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit JiggleMate from its menu, then drag it from Applications to the Trash. Optionally remove it from Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
