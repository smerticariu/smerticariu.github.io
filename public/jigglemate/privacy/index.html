JiggleMate

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

JiggleMate is a Mac menu bar app that keeps your Mac awake and active while you step away. It was built on a simple principle: what happens on your Mac stays on your Mac. This policy explains exactly what JiggleMate uses and where it lives.

The short version

JiggleMate does not collect any data. It has no account system, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no third-party trackers or SDKs, and it never connects to the internet. The developer cannot see anything you do in the app.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your settings — modes, timers, schedules, rules, the apps you choose for rules, click positions, your shortcut and menu bar icon. Stored locally in the app's sandboxed container.
  • Activity statistics — how long JiggleMate was active per day and how many times it jiggled, kept for up to 60 days to draw the Insights chart. Stored locally; never sent anywhere.
  • An activity log — the last few start/stop events, kept in memory only and cleared when you quit.

Deleting the app removes all of this. You can also export or import your settings yourself as a file.

Permissions and system information

  • Pointer control (optional). Allowed under Privacy & Security → Accessibility. Used only to post pointer movement, a tiny scroll, a silent key press and the clicks you configure. JiggleMate does not read the contents of other apps, the screen, or anything you type.
  • Idle time. JiggleMate reads how long it has been since the last mouse or keyboard input, so it only acts while you're away. It never records which keys you press or where you click.
  • Microphone and camera state (optional "In a call" rule). Only checks whether a microphone or camera is currently in use by any app. JiggleMate never opens, records or accesses audio or video.
  • Running apps (optional "Only While" rules). Checks whether the apps you chose are running or in front. Nothing about them is stored or shared.
  • Power, battery, display and CPU state. Read locally to pause on low battery, in Low Power Mode, when the Mac is hot, or for the rules you turn on.
  • Notifications (optional). Local notifications when JiggleMate starts or stops automatically. macOS asks for permission the first time one is sent.
  • Launch at login (optional). Only when you turn it on.

Purchases

The free trial and JiggleMate Pro are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. JiggleMate only learns whether your purchase is active; it never sees your name, payment details or Apple Account information. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

JiggleMate collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or stored by the developer, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. Everything JiggleMate keeps is on your Mac and under your control.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Any change that adds data collection would be announced in the app's release notes first.
