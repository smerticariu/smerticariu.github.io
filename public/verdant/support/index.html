Quick answers

What is Verdant?

Verdant is a gentle home-care app for anyone who feels overwhelmed by housework. Instead of a checklist, it shows you exactly one task a day — picked for you, sized to the time you actually have.

How does the free trial work?

Everything is unlocked for 14 days from the moment you first open the app — no payment, no sign-up required. After the trial, your daily task, Guests mode, streaks, and every comfort feature stay free, always.

Why did the app pick this specific task?

Verdant quietly weighs how long a task has been waiting, how much time you said you have most days, and what bugs you the most — then picks the single best fit. Tomorrow it'll pick something else.

What is "Guests are coming"?

Tell Verdant how long you have — 30, 60, or 120 minutes — and it gives you a quick list of only what guests would actually see, in the order that matters most. Free, always.

Can I add before/after photos?

Yes — tap "Add before & after photos" on any task card, then choose your camera or photo library. It's entirely optional and never required to finish a task.

What's the calendar for?

It's a look back, never a look forward — browse what you've actually done by day, week, month, or year. Verdant never shows you a list of what's "due."

Can my family use it together?

Yes — add the people who share your home under Settings → Your household. Verdant gives each person their own small step, sized to what they can do.

I missed a day — did I lose my streak?

No. A missed day gets a grace day, not a reset. Verdant never shows overdue counts or red badges — the rest just waits quietly for you.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed entirely by Apple. Go to Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions on your device.

Is my data private?

Yes. Verdant has no servers, no tracking, and no ads. Everything lives in your own iCloud via Apple CloudKit, and the developer can't read it. See the Privacy Policy.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone on iOS 18 or later. Signing into iCloud keeps your data backed up and synced across devices, but isn't required to start using the app.

How do I start completely over?

Go to Settings → Start over. This erases your rooms, tasks, and history from this device and iCloud so you can re-onboard from scratch.
