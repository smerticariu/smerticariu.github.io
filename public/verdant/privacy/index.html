Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

Verdant gives you one small step of home care a day — never a checklist, never a score, never a red "overdue" badge. It's built on a simple principle: your home is yours, and so is the data about it. This policy explains exactly what Verdant stores and where it lives.

The short version

We don't run any servers, we have no analytics, no ads, and no third-party trackers. Verdant has no account system of its own — it uses your Apple ID through Apple's CloudKit. Your rooms, tasks, history, and photos are stored in your own iCloud account. We, the developer, cannot read them.

What Verdant stores, and where

Verdant uses Apple CloudKit (iCloud) as its only backend. The following is stored in your private iCloud database:

  • Your home(s) and rooms. Names, types, and the rooms you've set up — you can track more than one home (e.g. a house and a vacation place).
  • Your cleaning tasks and history. What's been done, when, and how long it took, so Verdant can quietly pick the right next step for you.
  • Before/after photos, if you choose to add any. Entirely optional — never required to complete a task — and stored only in your own iCloud.
  • Household members you add. Just first names and a role (adult, teen, child) so tasks can be shared fairly. This is not an account system — nobody signs in.
  • Your subscription status. Handled by Apple's StoreKit; we never see your payment details.

Because this data lives in your iCloud, the developer has no access to its contents.

Camera & Photo Library

Verdant only asks for camera or photo library access at the moment you choose to add a before/after photo to a task — never on launch, never in the background. Any photo you add is resized and stored in your own iCloud alongside that task's history; it is never uploaded anywhere else.

Household sharing (optional)

If you choose to share a household with the people you live with, Verdant creates a private, shared iCloud zone that only the people you invite can access, using Apple's CloudKit sharing. We cannot see the contents of that shared zone either.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking SDKs.
  • No advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party data sharing or selling — there are no third parties.
  • No custom servers; we operate no backend that can see your data.

Your control & deleting your data

  • Start over anytime. Settings → "Start over" erases your rooms, tasks, and history from this device and iCloud.
  • Delete the app to remove it from your device, and manage or delete the associated iCloud data via Settings → [your name] → iCloud on your device.

Children

Verdant is a general-purpose household app intended for adults managing a home, and is not directed at children under 13. Household member profiles for children are just a name and a role added by an adult in the home — they are not separate accounts.

Subscriptions

Verdant Premium is billed entirely by Apple through your App Store account. We never receive your payment information. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and post the new version on this page.
