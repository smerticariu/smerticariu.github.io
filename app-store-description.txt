Battery Guard is the native macOS menu bar app that gives your Mac the battery tools Apple never shipped — custom charge alerts, real battery health history, deep usage insights, and desktop widgets. Everything is computed on-device. The app has no network access at all: no accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing ever leaves your Mac.

WHAT YOU GET

• Custom charge alerts — get notified at any battery level you choose, with sound, on-screen position, and a screen-edge glow you can't miss.
• Battery health, cycles & temperature — charted over time, with a clear replacement forecast so you know where your battery really stands.
• Usage insights — a charging heatmap plus runtime and standby stats that show how you actually use your Mac.
• Configurable menu bar icon — show exactly the readout you want, your way.
• Desktop widgets — small and medium widgets to keep an eye on your battery at a glance.
• Health certificate & export — generate a PDF battery health certificate and export your data as CSV or JSON.
• Auto-dismiss macOS's own battery banners — no more pop-ups fighting for your attention.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Battery Guard is built in native Swift — no Electron, no web view — so it's light on CPU and energy. It has zero network access, which means there's nothing to collect and nothing to leak. Every reading is computed and stored on your Mac.
